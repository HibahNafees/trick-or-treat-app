How to play

  • The doorbell rings and a visitor in costume is at the door. Kids and monsters wear the same costumes.
  • Each visitor hides one sign in one spot: the head, chest, tummy, an arm or a leg. It's in a new spot every time. Search with the scanner; it shows black until its centre is right over the sign.
  • A red skull ☠ means a monster. A green person with a ✓ means a real kid.
  • Drag your finger over the door and the scanner floats just above it.
  • Kid? Press 🍬 Treat. Monster? Press 👻 BOO.
  • In round 1, answer within 1 second for 100 points. After that the points drop fast, but a right answer is always worth at least 10. Visitors leave after 3 seconds.
  • One mistake ends the game: a wrong answer, or letting a visitor leave before you answer.
  • Every 5 visitors is a new round, and every round the scanner gets smaller and visitors leave sooner.
  • Need a break? Press ⏸ to pause.

Sounds are made live on your phone with the Web Audio API. No audio files are used.
